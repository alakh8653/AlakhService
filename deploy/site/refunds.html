If the Shop cancels your booking, or does not confirm your request in time (it then expires), you get a full refund of anything you paid online.

Problems with the service

If the service was not delivered or was not as described, raise a dispute from the booking in the app within [48 hours] of the booking time. We look at the booking, the chat and both sides' accounts and decide within [7 days]. If we decide in your favour the booking is refunded in full or in part.

  • We do not refund in cash, and we never ask for your card details, UPI PIN or OTP to give a refund.
