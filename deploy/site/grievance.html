How to complain

  1. For a problem with a booking, first use Help or Raise a dispute on the booking in the app. It is usually the fastest way.
  2. If that does not solve it, or for anything else (your data, your account, content on the Platform), email the Grievance Officer. Include your registered phone number or email, the booking number if any, and what happened.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and give you a reference number.
  • We resolve it within 30 days (sooner for content that the law requires us to act on faster) and tell you the outcome.
  • For personal data complaints, if you are not satisfied you may then complain to the Data Protection Board of India. For consumer complaints you may approach the consumer commission, or call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915.
