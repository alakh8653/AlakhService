# What customers and service providers need: research and roadmap

November 2026. A look at what leading service marketplaces and booking apps offer (Urban Company,
Thumbtack, TaskRabbit, Fresha, Booksy) and what surveys say customers want, compared with what
AlakhService already does. It ends with what was added and what is left.

Most salon and booking figures come from software vendors, so treat them as direction rather than proof.

## Where AlakhService already stands

Strong already: verified shops, hidden phone numbers in chat and masked calls, start codes, SOS and shared
visits, free fixes, disputes, the walk-in line and TV board, home visits with live tracking, pickups,
waitlist, prepaid packages, loyalty and referrals, tips, coupons, GST invoices, payouts, reminders the day
before and an hour before, English and Hindi, offline sending, and app lock.

## Added in this round

1. **Search filters and sorting**: price limit, 4★ and up, home visit, and sorting by price, rating or
   distance ("nearest first", with the distance on each result). Shops' locations and ratings now reach the
   catalogue. Browsing a type from the home screen also really filters by that type now (it did not).
2. **Repeat bookings**: "Repeat this booking" books the same visit every 1-4 weeks, 2-6 times, at the same
   time, through the normal booking checks; full dates are reported, never double booked.
3. **A reminder three days ahead** for visits booked more than four days in advance.
4. **Pay to confirm (no-show protection)**: a shop can ask customers to pay online when they book. An
   unpaid booking holds its place for 30 minutes, cash is not offered for it, and it is released (and the
   waitlist told) if not paid. Paid bookings are confirmed at once at shops that confirm automatically.
5. **Notification controls**: push, SMS and email each on or off, and quiet hours in Indian time. Every
   notice still appears in the app; being called to the counter and SOS always come through.
6. **Booking for someone else**: the customer adds the person's name; the shop sees "For: Asha Rao (booked
   by Ravi S.)". Only a name is shared, never a number.

## Still worth doing, most valuable first

| Idea | Why | Size |
|---|---|---|
| More Indian languages (Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada) | 57% prefer Indian languages; strings need native translators | Medium (translation) |
| "Open now" filter | The most used local-services filter; needs each shop's hours in the catalogue | Small |
| Instant / ASAP home visits | Urban Company's 30-60 minute offer; needs live provider availability | Large |
| Add to calendar | Fewer missed visits; one button on a booking | Small |
| Offers run by shops (e.g. 20% off weekdays) | "Offering a deal" is a top filter; today only admins create coupons | Medium |
| Help tickets inside the app | Customers expect a reply within a day; today help is FAQ, email and phone | Medium |
| Shops rating customers, blocking repeat no-shows | Protects providers; pairs with pay-to-confirm | Medium |
| Several services in one booking | Common in salons (haircut + beard) | Medium |
| Gift cards | Extra revenue for shops | Medium |
| analytics_service | It runs but nothing sends it events or reads it | Medium |
