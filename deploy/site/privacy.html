2. Purposes and legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you sign up, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows (for example, a booking you asked for, or meeting a legal obligation). We use it only for the purposes in the table above. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Your contact details are never shared with Shops

A Shop sees your display name (first name and last initial, e.g. "Ravi S."), the booking details, and your address only for a home visit or pickup while the booking is open. A Shop never receives your phone number or email. Messages go through the in-app chat, where phone numbers and emails are hidden automatically, and calls (where available) go through a masked number, so neither side sees the other's real number. Likewise, customers never see the personal numbers of a Shop's staff.

4. Who else receives data

Only processors working for us under contract, and only what each needs: our cloud host ([HOSTING PROVIDER, REGION]), Razorpay (payments and refunds), SMS and email providers ([MSG91 / TWILIO], [EMAIL PROVIDER]) for codes and booking updates, Google Firebase for push notifications, and, if enabled, our call-masking provider (Exotel). We may disclose data when the law requires it, e.g. to a court or a government agency with lawful authority.

  • Account data: while your account is open. When you delete it, your profile, addresses and devices are erased and your sign-in is anonymised at once.
  • Bookings, payments and invoices: kept under an anonymous id for 8 years, because tax and accounting laws (Income-tax Act, CGST Act) require it, and so the other party keeps their records.
  • Chat messages: [180 days] after the booking ends, unless needed for a dispute.
  • Security logs (sign-ins, IP addresses): up to [1 year].
  • Backups are overwritten within [14 days].

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access a summary of your data and how it is processed, and request a copy (in the app: Profile → Privacy).
  • Correct or update your data (in the app, or by asking us).
  • Erase your data: delete your account in the app or at /delete-account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Some features stop working without it.
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, access is limited to staff who need it, and backups are verified. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
